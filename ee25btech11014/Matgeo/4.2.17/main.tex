\textbf{Question:}\\
Find the direction and normal vectors of the line $y=x+2$\\
\textbf{Solution:}\\
The equation of the line can be given by
\begin{align}
    \Vec{x}=\myvec{x\\y}\\
    \Vec{n}^T=\myvec{1 &-1}\\
    c=-2
\end{align}
The equation of line can be expressed as 
\begin{align}
    \Vec{n}^T\Vec{x}=c
\end{align}
where $\Vec{n}$ is the normal vector of the given line.\\
\begin{center}
The direction vector of the line is perpendicular to $\Vec{n}$\\
Let $\vec{m}=\myvec{1\\m}$\\
\end{center}
We know that,
\begin{align}
\vec{n^T}\vec{m}=0\\
\implies \myvec{1 &-1}\myvec{1\\m}=0 \\
1-m=0\\
 m=1
\end{align}
$\therefore$ The normal vector of the line is  $\Vec{n}^T=\myvec{1 &-1}$ and the direction vector $\vec{m}=\myvec{1\\1}.$

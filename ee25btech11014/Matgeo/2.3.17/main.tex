\textbf{Question:} Find the properties of the triangle with vertices \brak{-4,0},\brak{0,3} and \brak{4,0}. 

\textbf{Solution:}

Vectors of sides of the triangle are,

\begin{align}
    \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{B}}=\myvec{-4\\0}-\myvec{0\\3}=\myvec{-4\\-3}
\end{align}

 \begin{align}
\brak{ \vec{B}-\vec{C}}=\myvec{0\\3}-\myvec{4\\0}=\myvec{-4\\3}
 \end{align}

 \begin{align}
 \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{C}}=\myvec{-4\\0}-\myvec{4\\0}=\myvec{-8\\0}
 \end{align}

To check for orthogonality, the dot product of the vectors of the sides of the triangle should be equal to zero.
\begin{align}
     \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{B}}^T \cdot \brak{ \vec{B}-\vec{C}}=\myvec{-4&-3}\cdot \myvec{-4\\3}=16-9=7\neq 0\\
     \brak{ \vec{B}-\vec{C}}^T \cdot  \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{C}}= \myvec{-4&3} \cdot \myvec{-8\\0}=32+0=32 \neq 0\\
      \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{C}}^T \cdot \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{B}}=\myvec{-8&0} \cdot \myvec{-4\\-3}=32-0=32 \neq 0
     \end{align}

\begin{center}
Hence, the given triangle is \textbf{NOT RIGHT ANGLED}.
\end{center}

To check if the given triangle is isosceles,a median should be perpendicular to a side of the triangle.\\
Let the midpoints of the sides \textbf{AB},\textbf{BC},\textbf be $\vec{x},\vec{y}$and $\vec{z}$.
\begin{align}
\vec{x}=\frac{\vec{A}+\vec{B}}{2}=\myvec{-4\\0}+\myvec{0\\3}=\myvec{-2\\\frac{3}{2}}\\ \vec{y}=\frac{\vec{B}+\vec{C}}{2}=\myvec{0\\3}+\myvec{4\\0}=\myvec{2\\\frac{3}{2}}\\
\vec{z}=\frac{\vec{A}+\vec{C}}{2}=\myvec{-4\\0}+\myvec{4\\0}=\myvec{0\\0} 
\end{align}
The vectors of medians of the triangle are:
\begin{align}
    \vec{x}-\vec{C}=\myvec{-2\\\frac{3}{2}}-\myvec{4\\0}=\myvec{-6\\\frac{3}{2}}\\
    \vec{y}-\vec{A}=\myvec{2\\\frac{3}{2}}-\myvec{-4\\0}=\myvec{6\\\frac{3}{2}}\\
    \vec{z}-\vec{B}=\myvec{0\\0}-\myvec{0\\3}=\myvec{0\\\frac{3}{2}}
\end{align}
\begin{align}
     \brak{\vec{x}-\vec{C}}^T \cdot \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{B}}=\myvec{-6&\frac{3}{2}} \cdot \myvec{-4\\-3}=24-\frac{9}{2}=\frac{39}{2} \neq 0\\
     \brak{ \vec{y}-\vec{A}}^T \cdot \brak{\vec{B}-\vec{C}}= \myvec{6&\frac{3}{2}} \cdot \myvec{-4\\3}=-24+\frac{9}{2}=\frac{-39}{2} \neq 0\\
     \brak{ \vec{z}-\vec{B}}^T \cdot \brak{\vec{A}-\vec{C}}= \myvec{0&\frac{3}{2}} \cdot \myvec{-8\\0}=0
\end{align}
 $\therefore$ We can conclude that triangle \textbf{ABC} is an \textbf{ISOSCELES} triangle.\\

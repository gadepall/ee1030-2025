\begin{frame}{Question}
Let $\Vec{T} : \mathbb{R}^4 \to \mathbb{R}^4$ be a linear map defined by
\[
\Vec{T}(x, y, z, w) = (x + z,\ 2x + y + 3z,\ 2y + 2z,\ w).
\]
Then the rank of $\Vec{T}$ is equal to \rule{2cm}{0.15mm}.


\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Solution}
    Let $\Vec{T}$ be the transforming matrix.  
    Now, take any general point $\Vec{x}$ in $\mathbb{R}^4$. We have
    \begin{align}
        \Vec{T}\myvec{x\\y\\z\\w} 
        &= \myvec{x + z\\ 2x + y + 3z\\ 2y + 2z\\ w} \\[6pt]
        &= x\myvec{1\\2\\0\\0} 
         + y\myvec{0\\1\\2\\0} 
         + z\myvec{1\\3\\2\\0} 
         + w\myvec{0\\0\\0\\1}
    \end{align}
\end{frame}
\begin{frame}{Solution}
    Therefore, the matrix representation of $\Vec{T}$ is
    \begin{align}
        \Vec{T} 
        &= \myvec{
        1 & 0 & 1 & 0\\
        2 & 1 & 3 & 0\\
        0 & 2 & 2 & 0\\
        0 & 0 & 0 & 1}
    \end{align}

    
    \begin{align}
        \boxed{\text{rank}(\Vec{T}) = 3.}
    \end{align}
\end{frame}

	\textbf{Question}:\\
	The determinant
	\begin{align}
		\mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ b & 1+b & 1 \\ 1 & 2b & 1}
	\end{align}
	evaluates to
	\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*)]
		\item $0$
		\item $2b(b-1)$
		\item $2(1-b)(1+2b)$
		\item $3b(1+b)$
	\end{enumerate}
	
	\solution
	
	Let the determinant of the given matrix be $\Delta$.
	\begin{align}
		\Delta = \mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ b & 1+b & 1 \\ 1 & 2b & 1}
	\end{align}
	We will use row operations to simplify the determinant. Applying the operation $R_2 \to R_2 - R_1$ does not change the value of the determinant.
	\begin{align}
		\Delta = \mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ b-(1+b) & 1+b-b & 1-1 \\ 1 & 2b & 1} = \mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 2b & 1}
	\end{align}
	Next, applying the operation $R_3 \to R_3 - R_1$ also does not change the value of the determinant.
	\begin{align}
		\Delta = \mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1-(1+b) & 2b-b & 1-1} = \mydet{1+b & b & 1 \\ -1 & 1 & 0 \\ -b & b & 0}
	\end{align}
	Now that the third column is in the form $(1, 0, 0)^\top$, we can easily evaluate the determinant by expanding along this column:
	\begin{align}
		\Delta &= 1 \cdot \mydet{-1 & 1 \\ -b & b} - 0 + 0 \\
		&= ((-1)(b) - (1)(-b)) \\
		&= -b + b = 0
	\end{align}
	The determinant is $0$, which corresponds to option (a).

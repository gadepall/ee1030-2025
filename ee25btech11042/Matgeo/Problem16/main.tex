	\textbf{Question}:\\
	A straight line L is perpendicular to the line $5x - y + 1 = 0$. The area of the triangle formed by L and the coordinate axes is 5. Find the equation of the line L.
	
	\solution
	
	Let the given line be $L_1$ and the required perpendicular line be $L$.
	The equation of the given line $L_1$ is $5x - y = -1$. Using the matrix form $\vec{n}^\top\vec{x} = c$, its parameters are:
	\begin{align}
		\vec{n}_1 = \begin{myvec}{5 \\ -1}\end{myvec}, \quad \vec{x} = \begin{myvec}{x \\ y}\end{myvec}, \quad c_1 = -1
	\end{align}
	Let the required line $L$ have the equation $\vec{n}_2^\top\vec{x} = c_2$. Since the lines are perpendicular, the dot product of their normal vectors must be zero, so $\vec{n}_1^\top\vec{n}_2 = 0$.
	
	Let's assume the normal vector for line L is $\vec{n}_2 = \begin{myvec}{1 \\ m}\end{myvec}$. We can solve for $m$:
	\begin{align}
		\vec{n}_1^\top\vec{n}_2 = \begin{myvec}{5 & -1}\end{myvec}\begin{myvec}{1 \\ m}\end{myvec} = 5 - m &= 0 \nonumber \\
		\implies m &= 5
	\end{align}
	Therefore, the normal vector for line L is $\vec{n}_2 = \begin{myvec}{1 \\ 5}\end{myvec}$, and its equation is $x + 5y = c_2$.
	
	The x-intercept, $\vec{a}$, is the intersection of line L and the x-axis ($y=0$). We solve the system by reducing the augmented matrix to RREF:
	\begin{align}
		\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 5 & c_2 \\ 0 & 1 & 0} &\xleftrightarrow{\,R_1 \to R_1 - 5R_2} \augvec{2}{1}{1 & 0 & c_2 \\ 0 & 1 & 0}
	\end{align}
	This gives the solution $x = c_2$ and $y=0$. So, the x-intercept vector is $\vec{a} = \begin{myvec}{c_2 \\ 0}\end{myvec}$.
	
	The y-intercept, $\vec{b}$, is the intersection of line L and the y-axis ($x=0$). We solve by reducing the augmented matrix to RREF:
	\begin{align}
		\augvec{2}{1}{1 & 5 & c_2 \\ 1 & 0 & 0} &\xleftrightarrow{\,R_1 \leftrightarrow R_2} \augvec{2}{1}{1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 5 & c_2} \\
		&\xleftrightarrow{\,R_2 \to R_2 - R_1} \augvec{2}{1}{1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 5 & c_2} \\
		&\xleftrightarrow{\,R_2 \to \frac{1}{5}R_2} \augvec{2}{1}{1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & c_2/5}
	\end{align}
	This gives the solution $x=0$ and $y=c_2/5$. Thus, the y-intercept vector is $\vec{b} = \begin{myvec}{0 \\ c_2/5}\end{myvec}$.
	
	The area of the triangle formed by the origin, $\vec{a}$, and $\vec{b}$ is 5. Since $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$ lie on the axes, the angle $\theta$ between them is $90^\circ$.
	\begin{align}
		\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}\norm{\vec{a}}\norm{\vec{b}}\sin(90^\circ) &= 5 \nonumber \\
		\frac{1}{2} \left(\sqrt{c_2^2+0^2}\right) \left(\sqrt{0^2+(c_2/5)^2}\right) (1) &= 5 \nonumber \\
		\frac{1}{2} |c_2| \left|\frac{c_2}{5}\right| &= 5 \nonumber \\
		\frac{|c_2^2|}{10} &= 5 \nonumber \\
		c_2^2 &= 50 \implies c_2 = \pm\sqrt{50} = \pm 5\sqrt{2}
	\end{align}
	
	There are two possible lines that satisfy the given conditions. Substituting the values of $c_2$ into the matrix equation $\vec{n}_2^\top\vec{x} = c_2$, the possible equations for line L are:
	\begin{align}
		\begin{myvec}{1 & 5}\end{myvec}\begin{myvec}{x \\ y}\end{myvec} &= 5\sqrt{2} \\
		\begin{myvec}{1 & 5}\end{myvec}\begin{myvec}{x \\ y}\end{myvec} &= -5\sqrt{2}
	\end{align}

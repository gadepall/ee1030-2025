	\textbf{Question}:\\
	The solution to system of equations
	\begin{align}
		\begin{myvec}{2 & 5 \\ -4 & 3}\end{myvec}
		\begin{myvec}{x \\ y}\end{myvec}
		=
		\begin{myvec}{2 \\ -30}\end{myvec}
	\end{align}
	is
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item $6$,$2$
		\item $-6$,$2$
		\item $-6$,$-2$
		\item $6$,$-2$
	\end{enumerate}
	\solution
	
	We can write given system in the form of an augmented matrix and solve using row reduction.
	\begin{align}
		\myaugvec{2}{2 & 5 & 2 \\ -4 & 3 & -30} &\xleftrightarrow{R_2 \to R_2 + 2R_1} \myaugvec{2}{2 & 5 & 2 \\ 0 & 13 & -26} \\
		&\xleftrightarrow{R_2 \to \frac{1}{13}R_2} \myaugvec{2}{2 & 5 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & -2} \\
		&\xleftrightarrow{R_1 \to R_1 - 5R_2} \myaugvec{2}{2 & 0 & 12 \\ 0 & 1 & -2} \\
		&\xleftrightarrow{R_1 \to \frac{1}{2}R_1} \myaugvec{2}{1 & 0 & 6 \\ 0 & 1 & -2}
	\end{align}
	
	From the reduced row echelon form, we get the solution:
	\begin{align}
		x &= 6 \\
		y &= -2
	\end{align}
	
	The solution vector is
	\begin{align}
		\vec{x} = \begin{myvec}{6 \\ -2}\end{myvec}
	\end{align}

\textbf{Question}:\\
The line segment joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to
the third side and is half of it.\\

\textbf{Solution: }\\

Let ABC be the triangle ,\\
$\vec{E}$ and $\vec{F}$ be the midpoints of AB and BC respectively .


\begin{align}
  \vec{E}  = \frac{A + B}{2}\\
  \vec{F}  = \frac{B + C}{2}
\end{align}

The line joining these two points is  


\begin{align}
 \vec{EF} =  \frac{C - A}{2}
\end{align}

The line AC is
\begin{align}
\vec{AC} = \vec{C} - \vec{A}
\end{align}

Hence ,
\begin{align}
    \vec{EF} = \frac{1}{2}\vec{AC}
\end{align}
Therefore the line segment joining the mid-points of any two sides of a triangle is parallel to
the third side and is half of it
